Privacy policy

Applies to Leonida Signal Android version 2.01 · Last updated 27 November 2026

Walkergames develops Leonida Signal. This notice explains how the version 2.01 app handles information. Contact walkergamestudio@gmail.com with questions about privacy or support.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your manually entered game progress, preferences, spoiler settings, saved map pins, Field Plans, notes, guide positions and feedback locally. It may retain map tiles and operational cache information. It does not connect to your console's save or automatically transmit your game progress to Walkergames. Local settings and progress remain until you clear them or remove the app, subject to backup behavior.

Objective screenshots and text recognition

If you choose an objective screenshot, its preview, crop and recognized text are processed locally. The app uses bundled Google ML Kit text recognition. Screenshot contents and recognition results are not automatically uploaded to Walkergames or sent to Google by ML Kit for recognition. Google's ML Kit SDK may collect diagnostic and usage information, including identifiers, app and device details and performance metrics. See ML Kit Terms & Privacy and Android data disclosures. You can enter objective text manually instead.

Map access, source links and network metadata

When you use the online HD Atlas, the app fetches selected public map tiles over HTTPS from GitHub-hosted GTADB data. The tile host may receive ordinary connection information such as IP address, request time and client details. This request does not need your personal game progress, saved notes or Field Plan. The app caches viewed tiles and stores a local host-pause deadline and reason to avoid repeated requests when a host throttles traffic. Third-party guide links open outside the companion and are subject to those websites' own privacy practices.

Account, ads, purchases and reports

Version 2.01 does not enable app accounts, advertising, purchases, automatic review prompts, remote signed-content updates or a public correction-report submission service. It does not include an active first-party analytics service. Feedback and correction drafts remain local unless you choose to copy them or send an external message yourself. The third-party ML Kit diagnostics described above are separate from first-party analytics.

Exports, imports and Android backups

You decide when to export or share a backup and where to save it. An imported backup may merge known progress after you review and confirm it; it does not automatically raise your spoiler boundary. Android may back up or transfer local progress and preferences under your device's settings. The configured Android 12+ cloud-backup rule requires encryption capability. Older devices may behave differently. A locally deleted record may remain in a separate system backup. Check exported files before clearing the app or switching devices.

Security and retention

The app's Android manifest blocks cleartext HTTP connections. Progress and preferences are retained locally while you use the app. Cache entries and local feedback can be managed through supported controls; Android app-storage clearing can erase progress. We cannot restore an unbacked-up local save. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to handle your request; do not include unnecessary personal or account information. Contact us to request access to or deletion of information you have supplied to us, subject to applicable legal obligations. Support correspondence may remain in our email service. You may contact us about retention or deletion of information you have sent.

Your choices and contact

You can decline screenshot selection, use manual search, manage local feedback, avoid opening third-party guide links and use available cached map data. You can request information about privacy or exercise applicable data-protection rights by emailing walkergamestudio@gmail.com.

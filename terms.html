Terms of use

Leonida Signal Android versions 2.01–2.23 · Last updated 28 November 2026

Independent reference tool

Leonida Signal is an unofficial reference and progress-planning companion, not the game itself. It does not connect to your game save or automatically perform game actions.

Accuracy and spoilers

Sources can be incomplete, inconsistent, platform-specific or outdated. Map positions and approach instructions can be approximate; confirm them in your game. Some records are reported by publishers rather than independently verified. Spoiler settings help limit what is shown but cannot guarantee that every external source is spoiler-free.

Progress and cheats

Back up your local progress and game save before testing changes or cheat inputs. Cheat use may affect achievements and saving. The companion's local records are separate from your game. Keep copies of exported files.

Third-party sources

External sources have their own terms and privacy practices. Citation and attribution do not imply endorsement or permission for unrelated material. See the credits for source acknowledgments.
